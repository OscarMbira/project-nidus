# One front door for the Project Nidus family

Project Nidus now has a single home page that introduces the whole family: the Platform for running
real projects and portfolios, the Simulator for practising project management safely, and soon the
Project Planner for teams that only need planning.

## What you will see

- A short introduction to Nidus and one card for each product, telling you who it is for.
- "Learn more" and "Get started" links that take you straight to the right product.
- A "Sign in" menu that takes returning users to their product's sign-in page.
- A slim "Nidus family" bar at the top of each product's own home page, so you can move between
  products without hunting for links.

## Why it matters

Most people arrive knowing roughly what they need. Each product keeps its own detailed page, and
the Nidus home page helps everyone else find the right one. When the Planner launches it joins the
family on the same page, and because Planner and Platform share the same projects, moving up later
never means re-entering a plan.
