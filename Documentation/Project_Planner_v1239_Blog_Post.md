# Meet Nidus Project Planner: scheduling without the heavy software

Not every team needs a full project management information system. Some want to plan the work, see who is overloaded and keep the dates honest. That is what the new **Nidus Project Planner** is for.

## What you get

- **A real schedule.** Give tasks durations, link them, and press Recalculate. The dates follow your working calendar, holidays included, and the critical path is highlighted on the Gantt chart.
- **Several views of one plan.** A task grid, a Gantt chart, a board, a network view, a look-ahead list, milestones and a due-date calendar, all reading the same tasks.
- **Workload.** See each person's estimated hours week by week against their capacity, and catch over-allocation early.
- **Time against tasks.** Team members log their hours each week and can see how they compare with the estimate.
- **Baselines and variance.** Save the plan, then see which tasks are slipping and by how much.
- **Bring your plan with you.** Import a schedule from XML, Excel or CSV, with a preview before anything is saved, and export any time.
- **Safe team editing.** If two people change the plan at once, the second save is stopped and offered a reload. Nobody's work is silently overwritten.

## Built on the same engine as Nidus PMIS

The Planner is not a separate product bolted on. It uses exactly the same planning screens as the full Nidus PMIS and the Nidus Simulator, so every improvement reaches all three at once. Your data lives in the same place too. When your team outgrows the Planner, upgrading opens the full PMIS — risks, issues, change control, finance and portfolio management — with every project, task, baseline and timesheet already there.
