# Team Charter Accountability (v1094)

## Purpose

Capture agreed **accountability** items (escalation, recovery actions, etc.) when
Team Charter norms or values are not followed — visible to all members on the charter view.

## Where

- **Edit:** Team Charter → tab **Accountability** (after Team Norms)
- **View:** section **Accountability** on the Team Charter page
- **Export:** included with charter content

## Data

- Column: `team_charters.accountability` (TEXT, newline-joined list)
- Apply: `SQL/v1094_team_charters_accountability.sql` (public + sim)

## Not included

- Formal acknowledgement workflow
- HR / discipline case management
